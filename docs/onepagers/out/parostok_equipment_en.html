Chemical-free microwave seed treatment

Parostok treats seed before sowing with a controlled 2.45 GHz microwave field. Dry process, no chemicals, no residues on the seed.

New design (rendering). The specifications refer to the current production model.

How it works

  • Seed flows continuously through a controlled microwave field.
  • The dose is set by exposure time at fixed power; each crop and variety has its own mode.
  • Treatment can be offered as a service or with the machine installed on site.

Evidence to date

  • Independent laboratory, April 2026 (Plant Health Institute, Ukravit Science Park; DSTU 4138-2002): 36 corn seed samples from 6 lots, 5 modes — uniform root development, no signs of suppression.
  • Weaker seed gains most: in the same test the lowest lot (86 % germination) gained 6.2 points, while lots at 90–92 % changed by −1.6 to +1.6 points.
  • Field, Poland 2024 (RSUW Głuchów, KWS Nataelo): 11.97 vs 10.34 t/ha (+15.7 %). Single plots without replication — indicative only.
  • Research since 2000 with Ukrainian research institutes: 24 crops in the 2000–2001 programme, replicated field trials on winter wheat.

For whom

Seed producers and processors, cooperatives and large farms — especially for seed lots below their germination standard.
