PAROSTOKDevelopment partnership

Gentle microwave treatment of tulip bulbs

Parostok builds on research into microwave seed treatment since 2000. Together with Belgian partners we propose to develop a dedicated machine for chemical-free treatment of living tulip bulbs — designed first of all not to harm the bulb.

Concept of the line

Functional diagram of the planned machine — not a drawing of an existing unit.

Why a dedicated machine

  • A tulip bulb (32–45 mm) is several times larger than the depth the microwave field penetrates (about 1 cm).
  • In round objects of this size microwave heating can concentrate at the centre — exactly where the flower bud sits.
  • Seed equipment therefore cannot simply be reused: bulbs need their own handling, dosing and control.

Where we stand

The effect of microwave treatment on tulip bulbs has not been demonstrated yet: we found no published study on living tulip bulbs. The programme therefore starts with bud-safety and pilot forcing trials against an untreated control, and stops if they show no benefit.

Design principles

  • Single layer of rotating bulbs, no drops or impacts.
  • Dose delivered in sections, with equalisation zones in between.
  • Hard limit: the bulb core never exceeds 42 °C. Hot-water treatment above about 45 °C is considered harmful to tulip bulbs.
  • Optional low-heat pulsed mode.
  • Mode library in non-volatile memory; AI selects and adjusts the dose within the qualified limits — the 42 °C limit is held by independent interlocks that the AI cannot override.
  • Remote monitoring and control: the line can run fully autonomously.
  • Every batch logged: power, temperatures, recipe.
  • Easy-to-clean contact surfaces, so that disease is not carried between lots.

What we are looking for in Belgium

  • Growers and forcers who can provide bulbs and forcing space for trials.
  • An independent research partner for the safety and forcing trials.
  • End users and co-funding partners for the prototype.
